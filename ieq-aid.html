Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services

How do I schedule a free legal clinic appointment?

Contact us at clinics@ieqaid.in or call +91 522-XXXX-XXXX to book. We offer appointments in-person and online, typically within 7 days of your request.

What if I cannot afford legal representation?

Our community clinics are completely free. For ongoing representation, we assess eligibility for pro bono services based on income and case merits. No one is turned away due to inability to pay.

How can I volunteer as a lawyer?

Email volunteer@ieqaid.in with your name, Bar Council registration, practice areas, and available hours. We'll match you with cases that fit your expertise.

Do you handle appeals or complex litigation?

Our clinics provide preliminary guidance and referrals. For complex cases, we connect you with specialized pro bono lawyers through our network.

What languages do you support?

We provide services in English, Hindi, Urdu, and regional languages. Request interpreter services when scheduling.

Can nonprofits and NGOs access your legal support?

Yes. Organizations working on social impact can contact ngo@ieqaid.in for governance, compliance, and contract review assistance.
