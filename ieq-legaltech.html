IEQ LegalTech isn't just a SaaS product — it's the revenue-generating intake engine of Ink & Equity LLP. Every document creates a billable legal touchpoint.

Automated Contract-to-Revenue Pipeline
Step 1
Client Submits

User describes deal on IEQ Contract Builder. Form captures deal type, parties, jurisdiction, governing law preference, and risk appetite.

Step 2
AI Drafts

Legal LLM generates a full draft in <30 seconds. Risk engine scores each clause. Stamping duty and registration fees auto-calculated per state.

Step 3 ✦ Key
Lawyer Review

Auto-routed to the Ink & Equity lawyer portal. Senior advocate reviews within 4 hours, marks approved/edits needed, adds legal opinion memo.

Revenue Touchpoint
Step 4
E-Sign & Seal

All parties sign via Aadhaar OTP or DSC. Ink & Equity advocate counter-signs as professional witness. Blockchain hash stored permanently.

Step 5
Lifecycle & ODR

Platform monitors renewal dates, compliance milestones, and payment triggers. If breach occurs, IEQ ODR activates. Lawyers at ieqlaw.com handle enforcement.
